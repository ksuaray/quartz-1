Consider an $m$-element sequence $(a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_m)$. If there are $n_1$ unique possible values for the first element, $n_2$ unique possible outcomes for the second element, $\ldots$, $n_m$ unique possible outcomes for the $m^{\text{th}}$ element, then the total number of unique sequences is:
$$
\prod_{i=1}^{m} n_i
$$
