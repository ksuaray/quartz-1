# Commutative, Associative and Distributive Laws

For two [[Definition - Event|event]]s $A$ and $B$:
$$
  (A \cup B)^c = A^c \cap B^c
$$
and
$$
  (A \cap B)^c = A^c \cup B^c
$$
This can be extended more generally to the set of events $\{A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n\}$:
$$
  \left(\bigcup_{i=1}^{n} A_i\right)^c
  =
  \bigcap_{i=1}^{n} A_i^c
$$
and
$$
  \left(\bigcap_{i=1}^{n} A_i\right)^c
  =
  \bigcup_{i=1}^{n} A_i^c
  $$
**Verify these properties using a Venn diagram!**
