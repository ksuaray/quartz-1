# Sample Space
## Definition
The set of all possible outcomes of a [[Definition - Random Experiment|random experiment]].
## Interpretation 

## Notation
The notation for sample space is $\Omega$ or $S$
Elements of a sample space (simple outcomes) are represented by $\omega$
