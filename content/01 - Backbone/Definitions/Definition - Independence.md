# Independence
## Definition 
Two non-empty [[Definition - Event|event]]s $A$ and $B$ are **independent** if:
$$
P(A \mid B) = P(A), \qquad \text{provided that } P(B) > 0
$$ and
$$
P(B \mid A) = P(B), \qquad \text{provided that } P(A) > 0
$$
Equivalently, when $A$ and $B$ are **independent**, then
$$
P(A \cap B) = P(A)\cdot P(B).
$$
## Interpretation 
Consider two [[Definition - Event|event]]s $A$ and $B$ in the sample space $\Omega$:
Two [[Definition - Event|event]]s are independent if the occurrence of one [[Definition - Event|event]] does not alter the chance that the other [[Definition - Event|event]] occurs.
The events are *dependent* if the occurrence of one event alters the chance that the other event occurs.
