Any ordered sequence of $k$ objects taken without replacement from a set of $n$ distinct objects is called a permutation of size $k$ of the objects. The number of
permutations of size $k$ that can be constructed from the $n$ objects is denoted by $_nP_k$.
$$_nP_k = \frac{n!}{(n-k)!}$$
