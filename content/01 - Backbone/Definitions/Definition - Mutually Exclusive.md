# Mutually Exclusive
## Definition 
On the other hand, two [[Definition - Event|event]]s are **mutually exclusive** if the occurrence of one event $A$ excludes the occurrence of the other [[Definition - Event|event]] $B$.
That is, mutual exclusivity occurs when the two non-empty [[Definition - Set|set]]s are disjoint:
$$
A \cap B = \varnothing
$$
and therefore
$$
P(A \cap B) = 0.
$$
## Interpretation 
Two events are mutually exclusive or disjoint if events $A$ and $B$ cannot occur together or simultaneously as an outcome of a [[Definition - Random Experiment|random experiment]].
